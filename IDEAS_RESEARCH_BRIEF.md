## 1. Environment-dependent physics ("same spec, different compiler")

**Closest published research**
- **Running constants.** The strength of electromagnetism (the fine-structure constant) is about 1/137 at everyday energies and about 1/128 at the energies of the Z boson, measured at CERN's LEP collider. The "constant" depends on the energy environment it is measured in. `SOURCE` *(not re-fetched)*
- **Screened forces (chameleon fields).** Khoury & Weltman (PRL 93, 171104, 2004) *(not re-fetched)* proposed a force whose strength depends on the density of its surroundings: hidden in dense places like planets and labs, visible in near-vacuum. Hamilton, Jaffe, Haslinger, Simmons, Müller & Khoury (*Science*, 2015, DOI 10.1126/science.aaa8883) tested it with single cesium atoms in ultra-high vacuum, because single atoms are less screened than bulk matter, and ruled out part of the theory's range. Jaffe et al. (*Nature Physics* 13, 938, 2017) *(not re-fetched)* extended this. Not fully closed. `SOURCE`
- **Constants varying across space.** Webb et al. (PRL 107, 191101, 2011; arXiv:1008.3907) reported a 4.2σ direction-dependent ("dipole") variation in the fine-structure constant of about 1 part in 100,000 (amplitude (1.02 ± 0.21)×10⁻⁵), from Keck and VLT quasar spectra. Disputed and unconfirmed. `SOURCE`
- **Chemistry changes with environment.** Helium, the least reactive element, forms a stable compound with sodium (Na₂He) above 113 GPa (Dong, Oganov et al., *Nature Chemistry*, 2017, DOI 10.1038/NCHEM.2716). Sodium, a shiny metal, becomes a transparent insulator near 200 GPa (Ma et al., *Nature* 458, 182, 2009) *(not re-fetched)*. Same elements, different "compiled" chemistry. `SOURCE`

**What fits the idea:** This is the best-supported part of the program. Chemistry demonstrably compiles differently in different environments, coupling strengths shift with energy, and a published class of theories makes a force's strength depend on its surroundings.

**What constrains it:** In every measured case the underlying rules stay fixed and the environment changes the output, like one source tree producing different binaries on different targets. For gravity, the "different compiler" test is whether bodies of different chemistry fall differently:
- Earth (iron-rich) and the Moon (rock-rich) fall toward the Sun identically to Δ(m_g/m_i) = (−3 ± 5)×10⁻¹⁴ (lunar laser ranging; Hofmann & Müller, CQG 35, 035015, 2018). `SOURCE`
- Titanium and platinum fall identically to about 10⁻¹⁵ (MICROSCOPE: η = [−1.5 ± 2.3 ± 1.5]×10⁻¹⁵; Touboul et al., PRL 129, 121102, 2022). `SOURCE`

A screened version of Idea 1 survives below those levels, or in environments not yet tested.

**Status:** ESTABLISHED for chemistry and running constants · CONTESTED for spatial variation · OPEN (narrowing) for screened gravity-like forces. **Next test: D1.**

## 2. Sound Logic: resonance shapes chemistry

**Closest published research**
- **Sonochemistry.** Ultrasound in a liquid makes bubbles that collapse violently. Multibubble collapse reaches about 5,000 K; single sonoluminescing bubbles exceed 15,000 K (Flannigan & Suslick, *Nature* 434, 52, 2005). This drives reactions that do not happen at room temperature: sound directly changes chemistry. `SOURCE`
- **Vibrational strong coupling (resonant cavity chemistry).** Thomas et al. (*Angew. Chem. Int. Ed.* 55, 11462, 2016) put a reaction inside an optical cavity tuned to one of the molecule's own bond vibrations (the Si–C stretch near 860 cm⁻¹). The reaction slowed by up to 5.5×, with nothing added but the resonance. A follow-up (Thomas et al., *Science*, 2019) reported that tuning could change which product forms. A careful independent replication saw the resonance but no change in rate (Imperatore, Asbury & Giebink, *J. Chem. Phys.* 154, 191103, 2021). The question is live. `SOURCE`

**What fits the idea:** "Resonance with the environment changes chemistry" is an active field with a *Science* paper, an unresolved replication dispute, and no agreed mechanism. It is the closest current match to Sound Logic and the best place to put the term to work.

**What constrains it:** Sonochemistry works mainly through heat and pressure from collapsing bubbles, not frequency matching as such. Whether resonance itself changes reaction rates is exactly what is disputed.

**Status:** ESTABLISHED (sonochemistry) · CONTESTED (resonant cavity chemistry). **Next test: D5.**

## 3. Sound and gravity are coupled

**Closest published research**
- **Sound carries gravitational mass.** Esposito, Krichevsky & Nicolis (PRL 122, 084501, 2019) calculated that a sound wave carries a tiny *negative* mass, so it drifts slightly upward in a gravity field. A 1-watt, 1-second sound wave in water carries about 0.1 mg; large earthquake waves might carry about 10¹¹ kg, possibly detectable by sensitive gravity sensors. Theoretical, not measured; its interpretation is debated (Gulevich & Kusmartsev, comment, arXiv:1903.04770). `SOURCE` (theory)
- **Acoustic-gravity waves.** In the atmosphere, waves are governed jointly by compression (sound) and buoyancy (gravity), and the field calls them acoustic-gravity waves. About 7 minutes after the 2011 Tohoku earthquake, ripples spread through the ionosphere at about 3.5 km/s, launched by sound from the shaking ground. Later ripples were atmospheric gravity waves launched from the sea surface near the tsunami source. Japan's ~1,200-receiver GPS network (GEONET) recorded them (NICT press release, 2011). `SOURCE`
- **Moving Earth's interior changes gravity instantly.** The same earthquake shifted mass inside Earth. The gravity change reached sensors 500–3,000 km away *before* the seismic waves (over two minutes early at 1,000 km), because gravity changes travel at the speed of light (Vallée, Ampuero, Juhel, Bernard, Montagner & Barsuglia, *Science*, 1 Dec 2017, DOI 10.1126/science.aao0746). `SOURCE`
- **Analog gravity.** Sound in a flowing fluid obeys the same equations as light near a black hole (Unruh 1981; research report, Q4). `SOURCE`

**What fits the idea:** Sound, the planet's interior, and gravity are coupled in several measured or calculated ways. The prompt-gravity result matches "Earth's innards" affecting gravity directly: shift the interior and gravity responds immediately.

**What constrains it:** Each coupling runs through mass moving or energy carried; none shows gravity cancelled or displaced by frequency. The sound-carried mass is the strongest lead for a direct acoustic–gravity signal, and nobody has measured it yet.

**Status:** ESTABLISHED (acoustic-gravity waves; prompt gravity signals) · THEORETICAL (sound-carried mass). **Next tests: D2, D3.**

## 4. Earth as an electromagnetic–acoustic organ

**Closest published research**
- **The churning core is a generator.** The convecting liquid-iron outer core produces Earth's magnetic field (the geodynamo). `SOURCE`
- **The planet rings.** After the 2004 Sumatra earthquake, Earth's "breathing" mode rang at 0.8146566 mHz with quality factor Q ≈ 5,500, ringing for months. Earth also hums continuously, driven by ocean storms (research report, Q2). `SOURCE`
- **The planet resonates electromagnetically.** The gap between the ground and the ionosphere is a resonant cavity that lightning keeps ringing at the Schumann resonances, fundamental about 7.8 Hz. `SOURCE` *(not re-fetched)*
- **Core, rotation and field are linked.** A 5.9-year oscillation in the length of day coincides with geomagnetic jerks (Holme & de Viron, *Nature* 499, 202, 2013). `SOURCE`
- **Ground, air and ionosphere are linked:** see the Tohoku observations above.

**What fits the idea:** "Electromagnetic organ" is a fair metaphor for what is measured: coupled resonances in the solid Earth, oceans, atmosphere, ionosphere and core, each with known frequencies and damping. Studying these couplings together is a recognised research area.

**What constrains it:** The test that separates "gravity comes from the churning" from the standard picture is whether gravity at a fixed station tracks the magnetic field over time. The field reverses, weakens and wanders. Idea 4 predicts gravity follows.

**Status:** ESTABLISHED (resonances and couplings) · OPEN (the gravity link). **Next test: D3.**

## 5. The atmosphere is shaped electromagnetically

**Closest published research**
- **A global electric field lifts the upper atmosphere.** NASA's Endurance rocket (launched 11 May 2022 from Svalbard, Norway) made the first measurement of Earth's ambipolar electric field: about 0.55 V across 322 miles of altitude (Collinson et al., *Nature*, 28 Aug 2024, "Earth's ambipolar electrostatic field and its role in ion escape to space"). It raises the ionosphere's scale height by 271%. On hydrogen ions its upward push is 10.6× gravity, launching them into space at supersonic speed (the polar wind). NASA called it possibly "as fundamental as Earth's gravity and magnetic fields." `SOURCE`
- **The magnetic-protection debate is real.** Gunell et al. (*Astronomy & Astrophysics* 614, L3, 2018) argue that a magnetic field does not by itself protect an atmosphere: Earth, Mars and Venus all lose roughly 0.5–2 kg/s, and a field can raise escape through open field lines at the poles. `SOURCE`

**What fits the idea:** Electromagnetic forces do shape the top of the atmosphere, a planet-wide electric field that competes with gravity was only confirmed in 2024, and what keeps atmospheres is still argued in the literature.

**What constrains it:** The measured field pushes charged particles up and out; it does not hold neutral air down. Below about 100 km, nitrogen (repelled by magnets) and oxygen (attracted) stay evenly mixed, which an electromagnetic hold would not allow.

**Status:** ESTABLISHED (ambipolar field; EM shaping of the upper atmosphere) · CONTESTED (role of magnetic fields in retention) · OPEN (EM holding the lower atmosphere). **Next test: D4.**

## 7. Tesla

Late in life (around age 81–82) Tesla wrote that he had worked out "a dynamic theory of gravity" explaining "the causes of this force and the motions of heavenly bodies," and he rejected Einstein's curved space. He never presented the details (David J. Kent, Tesla historian). So there is a trace that Tesla held a non-Einsteinian gravity theory, but no surviving text of it. `SOURCE` (secondary report of a primary statement)

- **Class:** SOURCE → DERIVATION.

### D2 — Replicate the prompt gravity signal from Earth's interior (days, workstation) → Ideas 3, 4
- **Data:** IRIS/EarthScope FDSN broadband data (networks IU, II) and IGETS superconducting gravimeters, 500–3,000 km from the 2011 Tohoku M9.1 epicentre; then 2004 Sumatra and 2010 Maule.
- **Method:** Follow Vallée et al. 2017's processing; detect the signal before the P-wave; compare its amplitude with their model.
- **Outcome:** a working program pipeline for "interior → gravity" signals. A mismatch with the model would be a real finding.
- **Extension:** estimate whether the sound-carried mass of Esposito et al. 2019 (~10¹¹ kg for great quakes) would be detectable in the same records. Nobody has measured it.
- **Class:** COMMAND.

### D3 — Does gravity track the magnetic field? (1–2 weeks, workstation) → Idea 4
- **Data:** long IGETS superconducting-gravimeter series, each paired with its nearest INTERMAGNET geomagnetic observatory; IERS length-of-day series.
- **Method:** Remove tides, air pressure, polar motion and hydrology with standard corrections. Correlate gravity residuals with field strength, secular variation, and dated geomagnetic jerks.
- **Supports Idea 4:** gravity residuals correlated with field changes at p < 0.001 after correction, same sign at most stations, replicated on held-out years.
- **Falsifies:** no correlation above the gravimeters' noise floor.
- **Class:** COMMAND → DERIVATION.

### D4 — Is the lower atmosphere held electromagnetically? (days, laptop) → Idea 5
- **Data:** NRLMSIS 2.0 neutral-atmosphere model; rocket and satellite mass-spectrometer profiles of N₂, O₂, Ar and He with altitude; the Endurance 2022 ambipolar-field profile.
- **Method:** Compute the vertical separation of species that an electromagnetic hold would produce (O₂ paramagnetic, N₂ diamagnetic, plus the measured electric field). Compare it with the gravity–temperature mixing prediction, and with measured ratios below and above the ~100 km turbopause.
- **Supports Idea 5:** species separation matching the EM prediction.
- **Falsifies (lower atmosphere):** constant mixing ratios below the turbopause.
- **Class:** SOURCE → DERIVATION.

### D5 — Sound Logic bench test: does frequency select chemistry? (2–4 weeks, roughly $2k) → Idea 2
- **Setup:** ultrasonic transducers at several frequencies (e.g., 20, 40, 120, 400 kHz); calorimetry to match delivered acoustic power; the standard potassium-iodide (Weissler) dosimetry reaction, in which sound-made radicals turn iodide into triiodide, read by UV-Vis at 355 nm.
- **Method:** Randomise run order and hold acoustic power equal across frequencies; measure yield vs frequency. Add a second reaction with competing products to test selectivity.
- **Known result to beat:** sonochemical efficiency already varies with frequency, typically peaking in the hundreds of kHz, because bubble size changes. *(not re-fetched)*
- **Supports Sound Logic:** frequency-dependent yield or product selectivity at matched power beyond standard cavitation scaling, replicated.
- **Falsifies the strong form:** results fully explained by power and standard cavitation scaling.
- **Class:** SOURCE (new primary measurement).
